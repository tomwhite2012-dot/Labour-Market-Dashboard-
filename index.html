UK labour market: key indicators

Five headline measures from the Office for National Statistics, each with its last five years. Figures are seasonally adjusted. Rates and wage growth are three-month averages as published by ONS.

Data: Office for National Statistics (ONS), Labour market overview, UK, released 20 October 2026; next release 17 November 2026. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Labour Force Survey estimates are subject to sampling variability and revision.
